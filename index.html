Explore Malappuram & beyond

Perinthalmanna sits at the crossroads of everything worth seeing in Malappuram district — temples, teak forests, hills and the Kerala coast.

14 km

Kadampuzha Devi Temple

One of Kerala's most visited Bhagavathy temples — an easy morning darshan drive.

4 km

Thirumandhamkunnu, Angadipuram

The hilltop temple famed for the Pooram festival, just outside town.

36 km

Nilambur Teak Forests

The world's oldest teak plantation, Teak Museum and Conolly's Plot river walk.

60 km

Silent Valley National Park

A full-day trip into one of India's last untouched rainforests.

28 km

Kottakkunnu, Malappuram

Hilltop gardens and sunset views over Malappuram town.

48 km

Ponnani Beach

Where the Bharathappuzha meets the Arabian Sea — lighthouses and fishing harbours.

Good To Know

Frequently asked questions

Where exactly is Happy Homes homestay in Perinthalmanna?
We are in the heart of Perinthalmanna town, Malappuram district, Kerala — about 800 m from the KSRTC bus stand and close to major hospitals, restaurants and the town market. Exact directions are shared on booking.
Is breakfast included in the room price?
Yes — every stay includes a freshly made Kerala breakfast (puttu, appam, dosa, idiyappam and more on rotation). Home-cooked lunch and dinner are available on request at a small extra charge.
Is Happy Homes suitable for families and women travellers?
Absolutely. We are a family-run homestay in a safe, quiet residential lane. Families with children, women travelling solo and elderly guests regularly stay with us in Malappuram.
What are check-in and check-out timings?
Check-in from 12:00 noon and check-out by 11:00 am. Early check-in or late check-out can usually be arranged if the room is free — just message us in advance.
Can you arrange local sightseeing in Malappuram and Kerala?
Yes. We arrange taxis and plan day trips to Kadampuzha temple, Nilambur teak forests, Silent Valley National Park, Kottakkunnu and Ponnani beach, plus longer Kerala itineraries.
How do I book a room at Happy Homes?
Call or WhatsApp us on +91 99619 59788, or use the enquiry form below. We confirm availability within a few hours, and payment is by UPI or bank transfer — no advance portals, no hidden charges.
